Research Statement

My research agenda seeks to investigate how, if at all, differences in CHIP protein function caused by STUB1 mutations directly influence cerebellar ataxias. Building on prior studies linking CHIP biochemistry to patient symptoms in SCAR16 and SCA48, I aim to analyze published patient data from varying demographics to analyze how much of the variation in cerebellar ataxias is affected from mutations versus environmental and genetic influences. By comparing signatures across mutation types and patient groups, I will identify which molecular changes determine symptoms and severity, and how other factors play into patient experiences. This research, through the reverse-transitional approach that uses patient data first before laboratory models, will contribute to a more accurate prognosis of what may be causing cerebellar ataxias based on patient-specific factors and biology.
